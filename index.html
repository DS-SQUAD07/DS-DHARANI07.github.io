AI · Multimodal Prototype in progress

FeelSync

Multimodal AI-Based Concussion Risk Screening System

FeelSync combines four kinds of signals into one screening risk estimate, then recommends further medical evaluation when needed. It is built as screening and decision support, not as a diagnosis tool.

How it works · pick a signal

The prototype uses live face and voice input. EEG and GSR come from recorded research datasets, so it is not presented as a true real-time four-signal system. The output is a screening risk estimate, never a probability of concussion.
